# Regulatory Context

> Design status: MVP — approved
>
> Implementation status: NOT STARTED

This page turns each legal fact the product depends on into a consequence for the system. It is research, not legal advice. Every row was read on 2026-10-31 from a page that was actually opened. Source quality is marked **P** for a Government or Ministry page, and **S** for a secondary source (law firm, accounting advice, software vendor, statute-text website). Where only secondary sources could be read, the row says so. Anything that could not be confirmed is marked **Unverified** with what would confirm it. The brief's own claims that were corrected are listed in [Decisions](00-decisions.md) as D-15..D-23.

## 1. Legal facts and their consequences

| ID | Topic | What was verified | Source | Status | Consequence for the system |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L-01 | E-invoices between businesses | E-invoicing is mandatory for VAT-registered sellers, B2G and B2B, "since 2022". Invoice data reaches the Tax Department directly or through an authorised service provider. | [Basware, Vietnam compliance map](https://basware.com/en/compliance-map/vietnam), last reviewed 2026-07-31 (S) | Confirmed (vendor page) | A domestic purchase invoice arrives as a structured XML file. This is why no OCR is needed for it, and it has been true since before the brief's chosen date. |
| L-02 | Current invoice decree and circular | Decree 254/2026/ND-CP (issued 2026-06-30, effective 2026-07-01) replaces Decree 123/2020/ND-CP as amended by Decree 70/2025/ND-CP. Circular 91/2026/TT-BTC (same dates) replaces Circular 32/2025/TT-BTC. | [EY Vietnam tax update, July 2026](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-va-chung-tu-dien-tu) (S); [Sapo summary of Circular 91](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/thong-tu-91-2026-tt-btc) (S) | Replacement is confirmed by the EY summary only; the Sapo summary confirms the dates of Circular 91 and that it guides Decree 254/2026. The official texts were not opened | The brief's constraint row is out of date (D-17). The system names its legal base as "the e-invoice decree and circular in force", with versions held in configuration. |
| L-03 | Coded versus non-coded e-invoices | A coded e-invoice (code from the tax authority) is the general rule. Non-coded e-invoices are limited to listed sectors that sell directly to consumers, including electricity, banking, insurance, healthcare, e-commerce, supermarkets, and transport, and they must meet data-transmission requirements. No revenue threshold is mentioned. | [EY Vietnam tax update, July 2026](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-va-chung-tu-dien-tu) (S) | Confirmed (one source) | Recurring electricity bills may be non-coded. The brief's High trust level requires a tax-authority code, so these bills would always become exceptions. Resolved by a trust level of its own, "Signed non-coded XML" (D-24); the usage-based limit is **open item O-16**. |
| L-04 | The 2025 cash-register rule | Decree 70/2025/ND-CP (issued 2025-03-20, effective 2025-06-01) requires cash-register e-invoices from household and individual businesses with annual revenue of VND 1 billion or more, and from enterprises that sell directly to consumers (retail, food and beverage, hotels, passenger transport, and similar). It says the cash-register invoice carries a tax-authority code or electronic data. Decree 254/2026 replaced it from 2026-07-01, and the EY summary says businesses selling directly to consumers may keep their registered invoice forms and are not required to adopt cash-register invoices. | [Government policy portal, Tax Department summary](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/mot-so-noi-dung-moi-cua-nghi-dinh-so-70-2025-nd-cp-ve-hoa-don-chung-tu-119250403074719995.htm) (P); [Sapo, the brief's source](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/hoa-don-dien-tu-la-gi) (S) | **Corrected** | The brief's sentence about all businesses with revenue of VND 1 billion is not supported by the Government page. It concerns sales to consumers, not purchases between businesses. The sentence is removed (D-15). |
| L-05 | Name of the tax authority | The General Department of Taxation was renamed the Tax Department, under the Ministry of Finance, on 2025-03-01 (Ministry of Finance Decision 381/QD-BTC, 2025-02-26). | [einvoice.vn news](https://einvoice.vn/tin-tuc/tong-cuc-thue-chinh-thuc-doi-ten-thanh-cuc-thue-tu-ngay-01-3-2025) (S) | Confirmed (one secondary source) | The documents say "Tax Department". Older decisions still carry the old issuer name. |
| L-06 | XML format decisions | Decision 1450/QD-TCT is dated 2021-10-07 and replaced Decision 635/QD-TCT; it is amended by 1510/QD-TCT. A listing also shows Decision 1271/QD-CT of 2025-05-30 on the same subject (data components of e-invoices and how they are sent to the tax authority). The brief's own source for the format is a 2021 article. Decree 254/2026 no longer sets invoice formats and leaves them to the Minister of Finance. | [Law-database page for 1450/QD-TCT](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue/quyet-dinh-1450-qd-tct-bo-tai-chinh-210675-d1.html) (S); [Legal-document listing for 2025](https://thuviennhadat.vn/phap-luat/tong-hop-van-ban-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-moi-nhat-2025-683095.html) (S); [iHOADON, 2021 article](https://ihoadon.vn/hddt/quy-dinh-ve-dinh-dang-hoa-don-dien-tu.html) (S); [EY](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-va-chung-tu-dien-tu) (S) | **Unverified** whether 1271/QD-CT replaces 1450 and 1510; the 1510 issue date is shown inconsistently | The XML adapter reads the schema version from each file; the list of accepted versions is configuration. **Open item O-09.** To confirm: the official texts and their "no longer in force" clauses. |
| L-07 | Adjustment and replacement invoices | An error in a critical field (buyer tax code, goods, amounts, tax rate) is fixed by an adjustment or replacement invoice. An error in a non-critical field (for example the buyer name) is fixed by notifying the buyer, with no reissue. One adjustment or replacement invoice, with a list attached, can cover several same-month errors for one buyer. | [Sapo summary of Circular 91, Art 10](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/thong-tu-91-2026-tt-btc) (S) | Confirmed (one secondary source) | Supports BR-31 (including the one-invoice-for-many case). BR-01 keys on the buyer tax code, not the name (D-26). |
| L-08 | Risky sellers | The Tax Department may classify a taxpayer as high-risk at registration (Circular 91, Art 7). MISA AMIS counts "more than 6,042" high-risk businesses as of 2026-09-22, compiled from several sources and not an official single list. If the buyer cannot prove the transaction is real, deducted VAT is reduced and the expense is not deductible for corporate income tax; invoices issued after the seller absconded give no deduction. | [MISA AMIS article, 2026-10-13](https://amis.misa.vn/?p=295073) (S); [Sapo summary of Circular 91](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/thong-tu-91-2026-tt-btc) (S) | Confirmed, with the correction in D-21 | BR-08 holds the payment for the Chief Accountant's decision instead of rejecting. The simulated risk list carries a source and a date per entry. |
| L-09 | Signature on accounting vouchers | The Accounting Law 2015 requires a voucher to carry "the signature, full name of the preparer, the approver, and the people concerned" (Art 16(1)(g)). An electronic voucher must carry an electronic signature or another form of electronic confirmation (Art 19(4), as amended in 2024). | [Ministry of Finance Q&A, dated 2026-10-22](https://portal.mof.gov.vn/hoidapcstc/home/cthoidap/166736) (P) | Confirmed | The AI never signs (BR-20). The signing screen shows preparer and approver names and records who signed what. |
| L-10 | Retention | Accounting vouchers used directly to post the books and prepare financial statements are kept at least 10 years, unless another law says otherwise (Accounting Law Art 41(5)(b)). A vendor page adds that e-invoices are stored electronically, including the human-readable version, for 10 years, and that digital signatures must stay verifiable over the retention period. | [Statute text, Accounting Law Art 41](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-ke-toan-2015/dieu-41) (S); [Basware](https://basware.com/en/compliance-map/vietnam) (S) | Confirmed, with the nuance in D-22 | The original XML is stored unchanged with a hash. The retention policy is designed in the MVP and not implemented, as the brief says. |
| L-11 | Accounting regime and form 05-TT | Circular 99/2025/TT-BTC (issued 2025-10-27, effective 2026-01-01) replaces Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC. An accounting-software page says Appendix I of Circular 99 still lists form 05-TT (payment request) and lets an entity design its own form where none is prescribed, provided it holds the mandatory content. Whether the Appendix I voucher forms are binding or only guidance is **Unverified**. The page the brief cites for 05-TT (dated 2026-06-18) still cites Circular 200. | [LuatVietnam on Circular 99](https://luatvietnam.vn/linh-vuc-khac/tai-thong-tu-99-2025-tt-btc-huong-dan-che-do-ke-toan-doanh-nghiep-186-105034-article.html) (S); [MISA AMIS on Circular 99](https://amis.misa.vn/251865/huong-dan-che-do-ke-toan-doanh-nghiep-tu-2026-theo-thong-tu-99-2025-tt-btc/) (S); [EasyHRM on 05-TT](https://easyhrm.vn/?p=6703) (S) | **Corrected** (D-19) | The consolidated request per supplier is the platform's own document, configured per entity (BR-20). It must hold the preparer and approver signatures, and it lists each invoice. |
| L-12 | VAT deduction and non-cash payment | A purchase of VND 5 million or more including VAT needs a non-cash payment document to keep the input VAT deduction (Decree 181/2025/ND-CP Art 26, effective 2025-07-01). Several purchases from the same seller on one day count together. A partly cash payment keeps the deduction only for the non-cash part. Cash deposited into the seller's account does not count. Imports under VND 5 million each are excluded. Whether a 2026 amendment exists was not checked, so it is **Unverified**. | [iHOADON](https://ihoadon.vn/hddt/hoa-don-tu-5trieu-dong-tro-len-chi-duoc-khau-tru-thue-gtgt-khi-thanh-toan-khong-dung-tien-mat-tu-01-07-2025.html) (S); [einvoice.vn](https://einvoice.vn/tin-tuc/nhung-diem-moi-trong-nghi-dinh-181-2025-nd-cp-huong-dan-chi-tiet-luat-thue-gtgt) (S); [Thuan Thien, updated 2026-06-16](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/?p=7811) (S), which cites the VAT Law as Law No. 48/2024/QH15 | Confirmed (secondary sources agree) | The payment step is tied to the tax goal. A signed batch is a non-cash transfer instruction by design (D-11). The cash flag of BR-32 (D-25) was removed from the MVP by D-33 and is Advanced (W-21); this fact stays as background and in the "why now" rationale. |
| L-13 | Imported goods | The deduction file for imported goods holds the customs declaration and proof of paying import-stage VAT (a payment slip or receipt). | [Thuan Thien, updated 2026-06-16](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/?p=7811) (S) | Confirmed | Advanced (W-20): BR-21, removed from the MVP by D-33, would require both documents before the invoice can enter a batch. |
| L-14 | Foreign contractor tax (FCT) on services | A Vietnamese enterprise buying services from a foreign supplier generally withholds and pays FCT for the supplier before paying; the expense file holds the contract, the foreign invoice, the bank payment document, and proof of paying FCT. A 2023 tax-office letter, applying Circular 80/2021 Art 81(1), says the buyer has no duty to withhold when the foreign supplier has itself registered, declared, and paid tax in Vietnam. Status under the Tax Administration Law No. 108/2025/QH15, which Circular 91 guides, is **Unverified**. | [Thuan Thien, updated 2026-03-17](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/kien-thuc/dich-vu-xuyen-bien-gioi/) (S); [Tax-office letter 86946/CTHN-TTHT (2023)](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue/cong-van-86946-cthn-ttht-2023-ve-viec-ke-khai-va-nop-thue-nha-thau-282325-d6.html) (P, an official letter from a local tax office on stated facts, hosted on a law database) | Answered for the 2023 rules | Advanced (W-20): BR-21, removed from the MVP by D-33, would block payment until the Chief Accountant confirms, with "supplier registered directly" as a possible outcome. **Open item O-07**, no longer applicable to the MVP. |
| L-15 | Purchase list for goods without an invoice | Form 02/TNDN, issued with Circular 20/2026/TT-BTC, is the current list from 2026-03-12 according to two vendor pages. A third page, updated 2026-03-17, still shows 01/TNDN under Circular 78/2014. The list supports the corporate income tax expense and is not described as giving a VAT deduction. The transition rule for the 2025 tax period is **Unverified**. | [Sapo](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/bang-ke-thu-mua-hang-hoa-dich-vu-mua-vao-khong-co-hoa-don) (S); [MISA meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/?p=51891) (S); [Thuan Thien](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/?p=10244) (S) | Answered for the form; transition Unverified | Advanced feature only. The sources conflict because pages differ in date. **Open item O-08.** |
| L-16 | Electronic signatures | A specialised e-signature must identify the signer and confirm the signer's approval of the data message, be tied only to the approved content, be under the signer's sole control at signing, and be checkable (E-Transactions Law 2023, Art 22(2)). Only a secure specialised e-signature or a digital signature is equal to a handwritten signature (Art 23). The articles read say nothing about delegation. | [Statute text, Art 22](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-giao-dich-dien-tu-2023/dieu-22) (S); [Statute text, Art 23](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-giao-dich-dien-tu-2023/dieu-23) (S) | Confirmed (text read); legal reading **open** | A platform e-signature with re-authentication plausibly qualifies if it is "secure". Approving by rule alone does not fit Art 22(2) on its face. One signature record per request, over that request's content. **Open items O-05, O-15** (legal sign-off). |
| L-17 | Personal data | Law 91/2025/QH15 on Personal Data Protection took effect on 2026-01-01. Art 20 requires a transfer impact assessment, sent to the specialised agency within 60 days of the first transfer, when personal data is stored offshore, passed to a foreign organisation, or processed on a foreign platform. The portion read has no general rule to keep personal data in Vietnam. Art 37(2) obliges a processor to work under a contract with the controller. One page that mixes enacted and draft wording was used for the article text. Whether financial or "important" data has its own localisation rule is **Unverified**. | [Provincial legal-dissemination page](https://pbgdpl.cantho.gov.vn/luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-nam-2025-bao-dam-quyen-rieng-tu-trong-ky-nguyen-so) (P, effective date and consent only); [Statute text, Law 91/2025](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-2025.html) (S) | Confirmed for Art 20 and 37; localisation Unverified | The MVP sends only synthetic data to an LLM API. Real supplier data (sole traders, bank account holders) would need the impact assessment first. **Open item O-03**, risk R-11. |
| L-18 | Tax portal access | The portal lets a logged-in taxpayer look up purchase invoices and download XML per invoice, with a search window of at most 31 days. No API for third parties was found in two further searches. Vendors advertise automatic fetching without describing how. | [MISA SME guide, 2026-10-21](https://sme.misa.vn/351069/cach-lay-hoa-don-dau-vao-tren-trang-thue-dien-tu/) (S); [xCyber BillStore](https://xcyber.vn/?p=2672) (S, a vendor claim) | **Unverified** (no official API found) | The MVP takes files by upload. The "Invoice Status Lookup Service" and a mailbox fetcher stay Advanced behind a connection point. **Open item O-10.** To confirm: the Tax Department's technical documentation, or a written answer from an accredited provider. |
| L-19 | PDF tampering | A guide warns that sellers may edit an XML or PDF invoice and send it by email or Zalo, and advises downloading the original XML. | [ACC Law Firm guide](https://accgroup.vn/in-tra-cuu-hoa-don-dien-tu) (S) | Confirmed | BR-18 (Advanced, W-19 and W-09): when the original XML exists, the PDF is never a data source. The MVP accepts XML only (BR-17). |
